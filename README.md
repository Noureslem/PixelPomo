# 🌿 About

**PixelPomo** is a cozy desktop Pomodoro timer inspired by the nostalgic atmosphere of classic pixel-art games like **Terraria - minecraft - stardew valley**

Instead of another boring productivity app, PixelPomo turns your study or work sessions into a relaxing retro adventure.

Pixel animations, soft colors and playful notifications make focusing feel like you're exploring a pixel world instead of staring at another timer.

---

# ✨ Features

## 🍅 Classic Pomodoro Timer

* 25-minute focus sessions
* 5-minute short breaks
* Automatic session switching
* Pause / Resume
* Reset Timer

---

## 🔄 Smart Automatic Cycle

When your **25-minute work session ends**

➡️ the timer automatically changes to

**5 Minute Break**

You'll receive a desktop notification:

> 🌿 **"Don't miss your rest!"**

---

When your **5-minute break ends**

the timer switches back to

**25 Minute Focus**

Notification:

> ⚔️ **"Time to get back to work, soldier!"**

---

## 🎨 Pixel-Art UI

Inspired by

* Terraria
* Stardew Valley
* Old SNES Games
* Pixel RPGs

Features:

* Pixel fonts
* Wooden UI panels
* Pixel grass
* Cozy colors
* Soft shadows
* Retro buttons
* Pixel borders

---

## 🎞️ Animated GIF Character

A pixel character stays beside the timer.

During work:

📖 studying animation

During break:

😴 sleeping animation

Idle:

😊 breathing animation

The character makes the app feel alive instead of static.

---

## 🔔 Desktop Notifications

PixelPomo reminds you exactly when it's time to switch.

Examples:

🌱 Don't miss your rest!

⚔️ Time to get back to work soldier!!

☕ You earned this break.

🔥 Stay focused. You've got this.

## 🎵 Optional Sound Effects

Pixel-style sounds when:

* timer starts

# ❤️ Why I Built This

Most Pomodoro apps are functional—but forgettable.

I wanted something that felt warm, nostalgic, and enjoyable to open every day. PixelPomo combines productivity with the comforting charm of retro pixel-art games, making focus sessions feel less like a chore and more like part of an adventure.
